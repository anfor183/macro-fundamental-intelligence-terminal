# Terminal User Guide

## 1. Navigating the Macro Terminal

### Dashboard
The primary command center:
- **Global Macro Regime Banner**: Displays the overarching regime (e.g. `RISK_ON | Disinflationary Slowdown`), growth momentum, inflation cycle, and central bank liquidity stance.
- **Top Conviction Signals Board**: Cards highlighting the strongest fundamental opportunities across covered markets.
- **What Changed Today**: Feed showing the latest bias upgrades, score movements, and catalytic triggers.
- **Asset Universe Table**: Filter by Forex, Metals, Commodities, and Indices. Click any row to open the comprehensive factor waterfall inspection modal.

### Currency Matrix
- Shows all 11 global currencies ranked from strongest to weakest.
- 11×11 cross-currency relative value table color-coded in green (positive relative strength) and red (negative relative strength).

### Forex Pairs
- Ranks all 28 forex pairs by fundamental conviction ($|\text{Score}| \times \text{Confidence}$).

### Specialized Gold & Oil Terminals
- Real-time factor decomposition for Gold (Real yields, USD, Fed odds, Central bank buying) and Crude Oil (OPEC+ compliance, inventory draws, China demand).

### Scenario Simulator
- Click the yellow **"SCENARIO SIMULATOR"** button in the header.
- Select from 6 high-impact scenarios:
  1. US CPI Downside Surprise
  2. US CPI Upside Spike
  3. US Labor Deterioration Shock
  4. Bank of Japan Surprise 50bps Hike
  5. Middle East Oil Supply Disruption
  6. Global Geopolitical Flight to Quality
- Click **"Inject Event"** to watch the quantitative engine recalculate scores and update the "What Changed Today" timeline in real-time!
